## Introduction

Dragon Warrior III is easiest to route when the party is treated as a long-term roster instead of only the four characters walking on screen. This guide starts from a stable main party, creates reserve characters early, and plans every class-related achievement before the first class change becomes available. That keeps the achievement grind controlled instead of forcing late-game detours after the story route has already moved on.

### Story So Far

Across the first two Dragon Warrior games, the series establishes a world where legendary heroes, royal bloodlines, sacred relics, and recurring darkness shape the fate of entire kingdoms.

In Dragon Warrior, the kingdom of Tantegel lives under the shadow of the Dragonlord. The Sphere of Light has been stolen, monsters roam the land, and Princess Gwaelin has been taken from the castle. A lone descendant of Erdrick crosses Alefgard, gathers the legendary tools needed to reach Charlock Castle, rescues the princess, and defeats the Dragonlord. With the Sphere of Light restored, peace returns to Alefgard.

Dragon Warrior II takes place generations later. The royal lines descended from that hero now rule Midenhall, Cannock, and Moonbrooke. When Hargon destroys Moonbrooke and unleashes a new wave of monsters, the prince of Midenhall sets out to reunite the heirs of Erdrick's bloodline. Together, the prince of Midenhall, the prince of Cannock, and the princess of Moonbrooke cross a much larger world, recover lost sigils, reach Hargon's stronghold, and end the cult's threat before an even greater evil can consume the world.

This story begins with Ortega, a warrior of Aliahan who crossed the world in pursuit of the Archfiend Baramos and never returned. Years later, on the Hero's sixteenth birthday, the king of Aliahan calls Ortega's child to the castle and lays that unfinished quest at their feet. The legends of Erdrick, the royal lines, and the old evils remain distant echoes for now; Dragon Warrior III will reveal what they mean in its own time.

### Class Achievement Plan

Plan the class achievements from the opening roster. The class-change system is unlocked at Dhama once a character reaches level 20, and spell-bearing characters keep learned spells after changing classes. The key idea is to teach the spell first, then change into the required physical class and cast it in battle.

- With a Little Help from My Friends: build a full party immediately in Aliahan.
- A New Line of Work: perform the first class change at Dhama once an eligible character reaches level 20.
- Paladin: have a Pilgrim learn Healmore, change that character into a Soldier, then cast Healmore during battle.
- Mage Knight: have a Wizard learn Blazemore, change that character into a Soldier, then cast Blazemore during battle.
- Berserker: have a Wizard learn Bikill, change that character into a Fighter, then cast Bikill during battle.
- So Much Magic: maintain two Sages at the same time. One should come from the Book of Satori route, and the other should come from a Goof-Off changing into Sage at Dhama.

The active party will carry the story efficiently while the reserves are rotated in during planned leveling windows. Later sections will call out exactly when to bring each reserve character out of Patty's Place, where to level them, and when to perform each class change.

### Achievement Routing Overview

Achievements will be handled in the earliest section where they can be earned without compromising the long-term party plan. Story and treasure achievements are routed by location, while class achievements are routed by spell-learning and class-change timing. If an achievement depends on a future class switch, the setup steps will appear before the final trigger so the roster is ready when the opportunity opens.

## Before Leaving Aliahan

### Section Achievements: Before Leaving Aliahan

| Achievement | Points | Trigger | Route Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| With a Little Help from My Friends | 1 | Build a full party. | Recruit three companions at Patty's Place after receiving the King's starting gold. |

The game opens inside the Hero's home in Aliahan. Your mother leads you outside and points you toward the castle, but you do not have full party control yet in any meaningful sense. Walk north into Aliahan Castle and speak with the King. He explains Ortega's unfinished quest, gives the Hero 50 gold, and directs you to the town eatery to gather companions.

This is the first point where party building is actually available. Leave the castle, enter the town, and go to Patty's Place in the northwest. The first-floor counter lets you add already-registered characters to the party. The second floor lets you create your own characters, which is the better route for this guide because the class achievements depend on planned spell inheritance and later class changes.

Create and recruit the opening travel party now: Hero, Soldier, Pilgrim, and Wizard. The Hero is fixed, so you are hiring the Soldier, Pilgrim, and Wizard. Put the Soldier in the second slot, the Pilgrim third, and the Wizard fourth so the sturdier characters absorb more single-target attacks.

> **Achievement Alert: With a Little Help from My Friends**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock after the third companion joins and fills the party.

Before leaving Patty's Place, create the reserve characters for the class route even though they will stay behind for now. Make one Goof-Off for the future second Sage, one Pilgrim who will eventually become a Soldier for Paladin, one Wizard who will eventually become a Soldier for Mage Knight, and one Wizard who will eventually become a Fighter for Berserker. You are not leveling them yet; this step is about locking in the roster plan while the route is still clean.

Aliahan is also your first safe base. Your house provides free rest through your mother, so use it instead of spending money at the inn while grinding the opening fields. Save your early gold for equipment and a small item reserve. The opening budget is tight, and a full party starts with uneven survivability, so upgrades matter more than convenience purchases.

Leave Aliahan by the western bridge once the active party has a few levels and enough gear to survive without running home after every fight. This section is the true departure from the starting island: first you reach the tower through the Cave on the Promontory, then you claim the Thief's Key, visit Reeve for the Magic Ball, and finally open the Cave of Enticement so the party can reach the mainland.

Do not rotate in the reserve class-achievement characters yet. The opening dungeons are short, but the active party still needs the experience, and carrying underleveled reserves here slows the route without helping their class-change goals. Keep the Hero, Soldier, Pilgrim, and Wizard together until the guide calls for planned reserve leveling later.

### Cave on the Promontory

Cross the bridge west of Aliahan, follow the coast south, and enter the cave at the end of the promontory. The cave is small, but it is worth clearing before entering the tower because it contains early money and a free armor upgrade.

Take the stairs down rather than the stairs leading back outside. On the lower floor, follow the path east into the larger room. The southeast room holds a Medical Herb. Return to the main path, go north through the narrow passage, and open the next chest for Wayfarer's Clothes. Equip them on the Wizard unless you already bought a set in Aliahan; if so, keep the extra set to sell. From that chest, take the northern side path to claim 16G.

After those three pickups, you can leave the way you came or continue toward the tower approach. If HP or MP is low, return to Aliahan and rest at home before pressing on. This is also a good time to buy several Antidote Herbs, because the tower introduces poison-capable enemies.

### Tower of Najima

From the cave/tower approach, head into the tower rather than detouring to Reeve first. Reeve matters soon, but the locked house with the Magic Ball requires the Thief's Key, so the key is the controlling objective.

The tower has an inn inside for 8G. Use it if the party is worn down after the cave, but do not treat it as mandatory; Aliahan's free rest is still nearby if you are willing to backtrack. From the lower approach, collect the 32G chest behind the locked wooden door after you have the key, not before. For the first climb, follow the tower upward, stopping for 40G and the Leather Helmet. Equip the Leather Helmet on the Hero or Soldier immediately.

On the upper floors, watch your status carefully. Babbles can poison the party, and the tower is long enough that poison damage can turn a manageable climb into a retreat. Use Antidote Herbs as soon as poison lands unless you are about to leave the dungeon.

Continue to the top floor and speak with the old man. Accept the Thief's Key.

> **Achievement Alert: Now You Can Open Doors**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock when the Thief's Key is added to the inventory.

Use the Thief's Key to open the nearby door, then drop from the tower edge to return to the lower section quickly. Before leaving, open the locked room in the basement area and claim the Agility Seed and Medical Herb. Use the Agility Seed immediately on the Hero or Soldier; the gain is small, but holding seeds only creates inventory clutter.

### Reeve

Exit the tower and enter Reeve to the north. Rest first if anyone is poisoned or low on HP. Reeve is your last proper town before leaving the island, so treat it as a preparation stop rather than a sightseeing stop.

The Magic Ball is the reason you came here. Go to the large house in the northeast, unlock the door with the Thief's Key, and speak with the old man upstairs to receive the Magic Ball. This does not trigger an achievement by itself, but it is required for reaching the main continent.

Spend your tower money carefully. The Chain Sickle is a major offensive upgrade for the Hero, Soldier, or Pilgrim, but it is expensive this early. The Magic Knife is a useful Wizard upgrade if you want the Wizard contributing more physical damage between spells. Leather Helmets are cheap and practical for characters who can wear them. Keep enough money for Antidote Herbs before entering the Cave of Enticement; Babbles are already a warning, and the next stretch still has status pressure.

### Cave of Enticement

Inside the cave entrance, walk to the sealed wall near the old man and use the Magic Ball. The blast opens the path to the real dungeon. From here onward, pits are the main navigation hazard. Falling does not ruin the route, but it wastes time and resources, so move carefully around the broken floor.

On the main dungeon floor, work around the first large pit by taking the side passage that loops to the north side. Pick up the Antidote Herb from the western dead end, then return and follow the eastern route. Continue east, then south, then west back toward the central halls. The Magic Knife chest is off the middle-left route; take it before heading for the stairs. Equip it on the Wizard if they are still using starter gear.

The lower floor is simpler. Take the left-hand route when the path splits, unlock the wooden door with the Thief's Key, and step onto the travel door at the end. The party arrives in a small shrine south of Romaly. Exit and walk north until Romaly is visible.

### Romaly

Enter Romaly from the shrine south of town and rest immediately if the Cave of Enticement left anyone poisoned or low. Romaly is smaller than Aliahan, but it introduces two things that matter for the guide: the first monster arena and the request to recover the Golden Crown from Kandar.

Since the achievement only cares that you pick the winning monster, this is the earliest point where `Winner!` can be earned. The lower odds monster is usually the safer pick, while high payout choices are riskier. Do not spend route-critical equipment money here; one or two attempts is fine, but a long betting spiral can set back armor and weapon upgrades.

### Cave West of Noaniels

The cave south of the Elf Village is long for this point in the game, but it is also generous. Its healing spring makes it the best controlled leveling spot in the arc. If you are behind, use the spring room to fight until the party is around level 12 or 13 before finishing the Dream Ruby route.

From the entrance, take the first western dark passage for a Wing of Wyvern. Continue south and east, then enter the northern dark passage for 288G. Farther in, descend through the southern route and pick up the Acorns of Life. Use them on the character with the weakest HP growth, usually the Wizard or Pilgrim depending on your rolls.

Work your way to the healing spring and refill HP/MP. From there, clear the northern side rooms for the Magic Knife and Strength Seed. The Magic Knife is likely a sell item if your Wizard already has the Poison Needle. The Strength Seed should go to the Hero or Soldier, where it improves every physical fight for the rest of the route.

On the lower route, collect 80G, 224G, Fairy Water, and 48G before taking the stairs to the bottom floor. Cross to the chest surrounded by stones and take the Dream Ruby. The attached message resolves the local story; the route objective is now to bring the ruby back to the Elf Queen. Cast Outside if available, or walk out carefully using the healing spring as a midpoint.

### Assaram

From Romaly, travel east and south across the bridge until you reach Assaram. The town is built around misdirection: the useful fixed-price shops are straightforward, while the haggling merchants start with absurd prices and only become merely overpriced after repeated refusals. Treat Assaram as an information and night-event stop, not as a place to drain the treasury.

Visit during the day first. The standard tool shop is safe, and the day armor shop has practical defensive upgrades if you skipped earlier purchases. The merchant in the northwest sells high-end items through a bargaining routine, but the final prices are still too high for most of this route. The second merchant marks up basic consumables, so ignore him unless you are deliberately experimenting.

At night, the center weapon shop opens and the theater becomes active. The night weapon shop is the important one: it sells Battle Axes at the normal 2500G price, far cheaper than the haggling merchant's best offer. Buy one for the Hero if you can afford it without gutting your Pyramid supply money; the Soldier can wait if funds are tight.

For the achievement, speak with the dancer near the center of town at night and accept the fortune-telling invitation. Follow her inside and complete the short event.

### Isis

The oasis in the southwest desert hides Isis. Rest on arrival, then check the shops before entering the castle. Full Plate Armor is the standout purchase for the Hero or Soldier, while Giant Shears are the big Soldier weapon if you have enough gold. Do not force these purchases before the Pyramid; the Pyramid itself pays out well.

Before chasing the Magic Key, take the hidden route to the Meteorite Armband. From the castle entrance, move left through the gap in the trees, continue north through the hidden wall path, and descend into the basement. ### Pyramid and the Magic Key

Enter the Pyramid around level 16 if possible. The dungeon is built to punish straight-line movement: several rooms hide pits in the center, while some chests are empty or are Man-Eater Chests. For the achievement route, you eventually want every chest opened, but survival still comes first. If the party is running out of healing, leave after the Magic Key, rest in Isis, and come back for the remaining chests.

On the first floor, walk north while hugging the outside walls of each open room. Do not cut through the center unless you are deliberately dropping into the basement. Continue to the stairs and climb to the second floor, then work around the narrow passages to the third floor.

The third-floor button puzzle uses the children's song from Isis. From the stairs, go east and press the button in the right-hand niche. Return to the stairs, go west, and press the button in the left-hand niche. The stone barrier opens. Enter the room beyond it and open both chests: Vitality Seed and Magic Key.

### Shrine of Dhama

From Baharata, go east across the bridge, north through the forest, and follow the river and hills until you reach the Shrine of Dhama. The shrine has no shops, but it has a cheap inn, a save point, and the class-change priest. You can inspect the class-change system immediately, but you should not make the main planned change until after Garuna gives you the Book of Satori.

Class changing requires level 20. A changed character returns to level 1 and keeps learned spells, while their existing stats are reduced. That sounds harsh, but the current region's enemies, especially Metal Slimes, let new classes catch up quickly. The important achievement principle is simple: learn the spell first, change into the required class second, then cast the retained spell in battle.

### Tower of Garuna

The Tower of Garuna is north of Dhama. Enter with the main party healed and with inventory space for several treasures. The tower is less about a boss and more about reading its rope-and-pit layout correctly. Falling in the correct place is part of the intended route to the Book of Satori.

On the first floor, go west through the blue-and-white tile area and collect the Intelligence Seed from the small room. Use it on the Wizard if they are staying active long enough to become the Book of Satori Sage; otherwise hold it for your future Sage plan. Return to the main path and take the outside stair tucked beyond the northwest wall.

Cross the rope on the second floor and descend to reach the teleporter room. The north stairs lead to 248G. The south stairs lead to a Man-Eater Chest if you want the fight, but it is not required for the route. Take the east stairs for the main climb, then work up to the upper rope. Cross to the west side to get the Iron Helmet, then return to the rope and deliberately step off near the middle. Fall through the pit in the landing room, descend again, and open the chest for the Book of Satori.

### Class Change Route

Return to Dhama with the Book of Satori. If the active Wizard is level 20 and has already learned Blazemore, use the Book of Satori to change the active Wizard into a Sage. This is the clean first change because it preserves the Wizard's offensive spell list and starts building Pilgrim magic on a character who can eventually help with healing and utility.

> **Achievement Alert: A New Line of Work**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock after the first profession change at Dhama.

After the first change, use Dhama's cheap inn and nearby enemies to bring the new Sage back up several levels. Do not leave the Sage at level 1 and immediately sail into harder content. The Dhama area is built for recovery loops, and Metal Slimes can accelerate catch-up if you get lucky.

#### Grinding After Class Changes

Class changes reset the character to level 1, so plan a short recovery grind before returning to the main route. The target is not to fully restore the old level number immediately; it is to rebuild enough HP, speed, and spell access that the changed character stops being a liability. For a newly changed Sage, aim for roughly level 10-12 before leaving the Dhama area, then continue gaining levels naturally through the next route section. For reserve achievement characters, grind only until they can survive a controlled battle and cast the retained spell needed for their achievement.

For pure level recovery, Dhama is the anchor. Fight near the shrine, take any Metal Slime attempts seriously, and return to the 8G inn as soon as the new class starts attracting danger. Do not grind new classes in the Pyramid, Baharata cave, or open sea immediately after changing; those areas either restrict recovery, stretch the walk back to safety, or punish low HP too quickly.

### Muor

### Tedanki

From Portoga, sail south along the coastline until you reach the river leading inland toward Tedanki. This town changes completely depending on time of day. During the day, it looks ruined and empty. At night, it appears alive, with an inn, weapon shop, and townspeople. You need both versions.

Enter during the day first. Go to the weapon shop building northwest of the entrance and head upstairs. The skeleton in the bed marks the town's tragedy, and the nearby chest contains the Lamp of Darkness. This item changes day into night when used outside battle, which makes future night checks much easier.

> **Achievement Alert: Night Time Anytime**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock when you take the Lamp of Darkness in Tedanki.

At night, Tedanki becomes a functioning town. Shop before resting, because staying at the inn advances time back to day. The north jail contains the prisoner who mentions an orb, but the guard and barred door mean you cannot collect it until you have the Final Key. For now, record the location, buy what you can afford, and move on.

### Eginbear

### Tower of Arp

The Tower of Arp is the next utility stop. The Echoing Flute is not required to know where the orbs are when following a guide, but it is an achievement item and a useful in-game confirmation tool. Enter with the Final Key. On the first floor, open the doors and reach the central room with four staircases. Take the upper-left stairs, climb through the straightforward route, and reach the fifth-floor rope maze. Along the southern rope area, the middle chest contains Clothes, while the side chests are Man-Eater Chests. Fight them only if you want the risk; they are not worth it for normal routing.

To reach the real treasure, move toward the center of the rope maze and drop through the largest central opening. If you fall correctly, you land on an isolated platform with four chests. Open them for Acorns of Life, Echoing Flute, 552G, and another Acorns of Life.

### Recommended Grinding Before the Next Orb Routes

After the Final Key and Echoing Flute, the next route gets rougher: Jipang has Orochi, Lancel's Navel of the Earth forces the Hero to go alone, and later orb routes push into stronger enemy tables. If the class-change section left anyone underleveled, stop here and recover.

### Navel of the Earth

Return to Lancel and enter the large shrine behind the village. This is a solo Hero trial, so prepare carefully before speaking to the priest. > **Achievement Alert: The Archfiend is Vanquished!**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock when Baramos is defeated.

After the battle, the party is restored. Leave the castle and Return to Aliahan. Speak with the King to begin the celebration, but the victory does not last. Zoma announces himself, the quest expands beyond Baramos, and the next section begins the World of Darkness route.

### Grinding Before Baramos

If Baramos is too punishing, do not grind inside his castle while low on resources. The Castle has excellent EXP from Metal Babbles, Stone Hulks, Evil Mages, Lionheads, and Marauders, but its danger is high. The Cave of Necrogond is a good midpoint if you can handle it, and Shrine of Necrogond/Liamland regions are safer than Baramos Castle while still offering decent rewards. Aim for the low-to-mid 30s at minimum, and higher if your Sage or reserve-swapped party members are behind.

This is the final route. The first half is short: collect the Staff of Rain, create the Rainbow Drop, and use it to bridge the channel to Zoma's island. ### Zoma's Castle

Cross the rainbow bridge and enter Zoma's Castle. Use Stepguard before damage floors when possible, and keep enough MP for the boss gauntlet. From the entrance, go north to the statues, then follow the long route east, north, west, and south until you reach the central throne room door. When you enter the throne room, the exits seal.

The statue trap sends three forced fights of two Granite Titans each. Heal between waves if needed; these are not throwaway enemies.

##### Standard Route: Sphere of Light

Use the Sphere of Light immediately. Then prioritize healing. Zoma can wipe away buffs, so do not over-invest in setup that can vanish. Use the Sage's Stone every round, use Healusall only when the party is near collapse, and keep the Hero and Soldier attacking whenever safe. Bikill can speed the fight, but reapply it only when healing is stable.

##### Challenge Route: No Sphere of Light

Do not use the Sphere of Light. The same fundamentals apply, but resource management is stricter. Enter with extra Wizard's Rings, revival items, and a party strong enough to survive extended turns without relying on fragile buffs. Treat this as a prepared challenge after learning the final gauntlet on the standard route.

> **Achievement Alert: The Foretelling**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock when Zoma is defeated.

> **Achievement Alert: We're Not Scared**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock only if Zoma is defeated without using the Sphere of Light during the fight.

### Epilogue

After Zoma falls, leave the altar area. The castle begins to collapse, and the party is thrown back through the cave northwest of Tantegel. Magic remains sealed during the escape, but there are no random encounters, so walk out carefully and return to Tantegel Castle.

Speak with the King and watch the ending sequence. This completes the story and the main achievement route.

> **Achievement Alert: A Legend is Born**
>
> **Trigger:** The achievement should unlock when the epilogue is viewed.

### Final Grinding Notes

The best final grinding target is Metal Babble near Rimuldar or inside late dungeons, worth 53600 EXP in the current reference table. The Silver Harp can force encounters when you are ready to farm, but use it near a reliable recovery point rather than deep inside Zoma's Castle. If you still need money, Gold Bashers offer 1228G, while high-end randoms such as Salamander, Putregon, Barog, Swordoid, and Archmage provide strong EXP but can end a weak run quickly.

Once Zoma is defeated and the epilogue has played, the story route is complete.
